Privacy Policy

the cure · Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the the cure Discord application (“the App”) handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Receive

the cure may receive information provided by Discord when you interact with the App, such as your Discord user ID, server ID, channel information, and information necessary to process commands. The App does not intentionally collect passwords, payment card information, or Discord account credentials.

2. How We Use Information

Information received by the App may be used only to provide and operate the App's features, process Discord commands, play and manage music, maintain and troubleshoot the App, and prevent abuse and technical problems.

3. Stored Information

the cure may temporarily process information required for active commands and music sessions. We do not intentionally sell or rent personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may interact with third-party services to provide certain features, including music-related functionality. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

6. Data Retention

Information that is no longer required for the operation of the App should not be retained longer than necessary.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended to operate through Discord and does not intentionally collect personal information directly from children.

8. Your Rights

If you have questions about information processed by the App or wish to request deletion of information held by us, contact us through the App's designated support server.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact us through the App's designated support server.
